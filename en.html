Train Lost & Found: Ride Log

Log the trains and buses you ride with one tap when you board. If you leave something behind, the app shows what lost and found will ask for: date and time, line, section and car

Frequently asked questions

How do I log a ride?
On the Now tab, add a line button for a line you ride (type any name). Tap the button when you board to log the ride with the current time. Tap Get off when you leave to save the exit time. If you forget, the ride is still kept.

How do I save the car number?
After you log a ride, buttons for cars 1 to 16 appear. One tap saves it (optional). A car you've chosen twice or more on the same line is suggested first. Use "Other number or letter" for coaches with letters or longer numbers. You can also save the door number and where you were.

How do I save a favorite route?
On the Now tab, tap Add next to Favorite routes and enter a name and the legs (line, boarding station, exit station). Use "Add a transfer" for more legs. After that, just tap Board and Get off on the route card. You can also save a route from a ride's details in History. When you ride the same way three times, the app offers to save it.

What do I do when I lose something?
Tap "I lost something", choose the ride and the item, and the app makes an inquiry card with the date and time, line, section, car and position, and the item's details. Copy or share it, or tap "Show to station staff" for a large, clear screen. If you're still at the station, tell the nearest staff or crew member right away.

Who do I contact?
"Where to ask" on the inquiry card helps you find the operator's lost and found. Check the operator's official page for hours and how to inquire. If the item isn't found, report it to the police as well. The app can't guarantee that a lost item will be found.

Does it work outside Japan?
Manual logging works for trains, buses, trams and ferries anywhere in the world. Type any line, station or stop name; if you allow location access, nearby stations and stops are suggested. Automatic logging is for trains in Japan only.

How does automatic logging work?
In Settings, open "How to log rides", choose Automatic and allow location access Always. The app uses your location only near stations and while you ride to save the boarding station, exit station, times and line. It can get things wrong underground, where lines run side by side, or in a car or bus near the tracks. You can fix the line or delete the ride in its details. Switch back to Manual any time if you're worried about battery life.

Does it show which train it was?
For rides on Toei Subway lines, the app estimates the train from the timetable (with delays it may be the one before or after; you can change it in the ride's details). JR and most private railways aren't covered because their timetable data isn't published for use in products. Timetable information is based on data from the Public Transportation Open Data Center and is not guaranteed to be accurate or complete. Please don't contact railway operators about what the app displays.

Widgets and Siri
The Home Screen widgets Favorite routes and Line buttons let you board, get off and set your car without opening the app. The Shortcuts app has Log a Ride, Log Getting Off, Log Car Number and Last Ride.

Where is my data stored?
Only on your device and in your own iCloud (when iCloud sync is on). The developer never receives it. With iCloud sync, you can check your latest ride on an iPad signed in to the same Apple Account even if you leave your iPhone behind.

How do I delete rides?
Use Delete in a ride's details. In Settings, "Keep rides for" can also delete older rides automatically.

Colors and the background pattern
Pick one of 9 colors in Settings, then Theme color. Turn off "Background pattern" for a plain background.

How do I remove ads?
Buy "Remove ads" in Settings (one-time purchase). On a new device, use "Restore purchase" on the same screen. Full-screen ads appear only occasionally at natural breaks, such as after closing a ride's details. They never appear when you tap Board, Get off or a car button, or while you're making a lost item inquiry.

Seeing an inappropriate ad?
Tap the ⓘ (AdChoices) mark in the corner of the ad to report it to Google, which serves the ads. You can also tell us the date and what the ad showed using the contact form below. Settings, then "Report an inappropriate ad", opens this page.
