Train Lost & Found: Ride Log Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This policy explains how user information is handled in the iOS app "Train Lost & Found: Ride Log" (the "App"; its Japanese name is 忘れ物した時のために！). The App is developed and operated by nekotatamao.

1. Information collected by the developer

The developer does not collect personal information such as your name, contact details, location or ride log through the App. The developer operates no servers or analytics for the App. However, as described in "6. Advertising", Google collects information in order to serve ads.

If you contact us through the form in "10. Contact", the name, email address and message you enter are used only to reply to you. The form uses Google Forms, and its contents are stored on Google's services.

2. Your ride log

Your rides (line, boarding and exit stations, times, car, door and position, train name, notes, and for automatically logged rides the stations passed and the path), favorite routes, line buttons, lost item records, items, and settings such as language, theme color and how rides are logged are stored on your device. When iCloud sync is on, they are also stored in your own iCloud (Apple's CloudKit private database) and synced between devices signed in to the same Apple Account. None of this is sent to the developer. The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets only show a summary of the log stored on your device.

3. Location and motion

  • When you tap Board or Get off, the App gets your current location once and saves the nearest station or stop as where you got on or off (location access "While Using the App"). You can turn this off in Settings > "Save the nearest station on Board and Get off". If it is off, or location access is not allowed, manual logging does not use your location.
  • When you add a line or route and tap "Find nearby stations and stops" or "Choose from lines at nearby stations", the App gets your current location once and looks for nearby stations and stops in the station data on your device and with Apple's Maps service (MapKit). This uses "While Using the App" location access.
  • Only if you choose Automatic logging, the App uses "Always" location access. It uses iOS region monitoring and significant-change location to notice when you enter or leave the area around a nearby station, gets your location only near stations and while you ride, compares it with the station and line data on your device, and saves the boarding station, exit station, times, line and path to your ride log. Switching back to Manual stops this.
  • With Automatic logging, the App may use your device's motion activity (Motion & Fitness) to tell when you get off a train and start walking. It is used only on your device and is neither stored nor sent. The App works without it.
  • Location is used only on your device (and in the ride log in your own iCloud) and is never sent to the developer or used for ads. When looking for nearby stations and stops, the area around your location is sent to Apple (subject to Apple's privacy policy).

4. Timetable lookups

For rides on supported lines (such as Toei Subway), the App requests that station's timetable from the Public Transportation Open Data Center (operated by the Association for Open Data of Public Transportation) to estimate which train you took. Only station and line identifiers are sent. Nothing that identifies you, and not the time of your ride, is sent (network information such as your IP address reaches their server as with any connection).

5. Finding where to ask

When you tap "Search the web for lost and found" on an inquiry card, the App opens a Google search in your browser using the operator's or line's name as the search words. This happens only when you tap it, and details such as the date and time of your ride are not sent.

6. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. To serve ads, measure their effectiveness, prevent fraud and confirm ad consent, AdMob may collect and use information such as the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow it), device identifiers limited to the App or developer (such as IDFV, which may be used even if you don't allow tracking), your approximate region inferred from your IP address, your device type and OS version, app launches and ad impressions and taps, and diagnostics about app performance and crashes. Location obtained by the App and your ride log are never used for ads. Widgets do not show ads.

  • Tracking for personalized ads happens only if you choose "Allow" in the iOS "Allow … to track" dialog. Every feature of the App works even if you don't allow it. You can stop tracking at any time by turning the App off in iOS Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking.
  • For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ad consent is confirmed with Google's consent management tool. Users in certain US states can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for personalized ads. You can change or withdraw consent, or opt out, at any time from Settings, then "Ad privacy settings" in the App (shown only to users in the regions concerned).
  • Google handles information under its own privacy policy. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Notifications

When "Ask for the car when I board" or "Remind me to check my items after I get off" is on, notifications are shown on your device. Their contents are not sent to the developer.

8. Paid feature (Remove Ads)

Purchasing and restoring "Remove ads" is handled through Apple's App Store. The developer does not receive your payment information.

9. Deleting data, and users in Europe and elsewhere

You can delete rides, favorite routes, line buttons, lost item records and items from their screens at any time. In Settings, "Keep rides for" can also delete older rides automatically. Deleting the App deletes the data on your device. Data stored in iCloud can be deleted from iOS Settings, then your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage. Apart from information received through the contact form, the developer holds no user data. Requests to access or delete information handled by Google for advertising can be made to Google as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: information is handled for advertising mainly on the basis of the consent you give in the consent management tool (some purposes may rely on the legitimate interests of Google and others shown in that tool, to which you can object on the same screen). You can withdraw consent at any time from Settings, then "Ad privacy settings" in the App. Information for advertising is sent to Google LLC (United States) and handled under the safeguards Google provides. You have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of your information, restriction of or objection to its processing, and data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

10. Contact and changes to this policy

For questions about the App, please use the contact form. Changes to this policy will be announced on this page.
